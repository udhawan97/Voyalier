Voyalier is a local-first, evidence-backed trip workspace. It brings scattered
confirmations, research, readiness, and traveler-authored plans into a reviewed
Blueprint, a quiet Today view, and a redaction-first brief.

![The current-source Voyalier Kyoto sample workspace with Today, pending suggestions, and a deterministic Blueprint.](/Voyalier/assets/screenshots/voyalier-trip-workspace.jpg)

## The simple version

1. Create a trip.
2. Bring in a confirmation or add your own plan.
3. Review every suggested fact against its source.
4. Use deterministic checks to find overlaps, gaps, and missing review.
5. Add only the connected context you ask for, then keep the useful result
   locally.

![Voyalier’s product flow from source through review, planning, preparation, and travel day.](/Voyalier/assets/voyalier-trip-workspace-flow.svg)

## What already works

The current source tree covers trip creation and editing; confirmation import
from pasted text, HTML, raw email, or local files; human review; deterministic
planning and readiness; official-advice and weather snapshots; research links
and files; offline city packs, recommendations, saved places, packing, manual
activities, rail, transfers, and maps; workspace search; Today; English and
Spanish; an encrypted vault; backup and restore; redacted sharing; and verified
in-app updates.

Optional Ollama, OpenAI, and Anthropic assistance sits beside that workflow. A
remote request requires explicit consent and an exact preview of the redacted
content leaving the device. Trip chat is on-device only and uses the local
Ollama path.

## What review looks like

![Voyalier’s review dialog showing local warning, fact-type, and extraction-method filters above a fictional candidate with quoted source evidence.](/Voyalier/assets/screenshots/voyalier-evidence-review.jpg)

Imported documents, retrieved pages, provider output, and model output are
untrusted. A parser may suggest a flight or stay, but it remains a candidate
until the traveler confirms or corrects it. Source spans, retrieval time,
license, and confidence stay attached where they matter.

## Where authority stops

![Voyalier’s visa and entry preparation cockpit with a clear non-authority warning and an official-source link.](/Voyalier/assets/screenshots/voyalier-visa-cockpit.jpg)

Voyalier does not decide whether you need a visa, declare a destination safe,
give medical clearance, guarantee a price, confirm availability, or promise an
opening time. It can organize a preparation route and point to a dated official
source; the traveler still confirms the real case with that authority.

## Product promise

- Useful deterministic planning without an AI key.
- Optional on-device or bring-your-own-key intelligence.
- Sources, freshness, confidence, and unknown states attached to factual claims.
- Explicit review before extracted details change a trip.
- Redaction before sharing.
- No telemetry, account, or silent background refresh.
