---
title: Save a trip before the dates are known
description: Keep an undated route as a local idea, then convert it into a dated trip when the window is real.
---

Choose **Save trip idea** after entering the origin and destination. Voyalier
keeps the dates empty and does not run an inventory search. The idea appears on
the Trips screen with two date fields. Add a valid start and end date, then
choose **Build dated trip**. Conversion creates the trip and removes the idea in
one local database transaction.

This is useful for an undecided Chicago to Montréal journey. It does not borrow
dates from another trip or imply that flights and rooms are available.
