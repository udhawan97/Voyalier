---
title: Choose an island or neighborhood
description: Compare destination areas by fit and tradeoff before narrowing provider links.
---

Open **Areas and islands** in the cockpit. Each card names a useful fit, a
tradeoff, and the destination authority used for orientation. Verify the source,
then choose the area that matches the trip. Voyalier updates the provider brief
and marks dependent stay or transport work for review.

Area cards are orientation, not live rankings. They do not claim current price,
availability, safety, opening hours, or a universal “best” choice.
