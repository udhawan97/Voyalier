---
title: Track trip money in exact currencies
description: Separate estimates, committed amounts, and refunds without inventing a converted total.
---

Enter the amount in its source currency and choose Estimate, Committed, or
Refund. Voyalier follows that currency’s minor-unit precision: JPY has no
decimal places, USD has two, and KWD has three. Extra precision and values too
large for exact local storage are refused.

Totals stay separated by currency. Unknown tax, fees, exchange rates, and
per-person versus party meaning remain visible instead of being guessed.
