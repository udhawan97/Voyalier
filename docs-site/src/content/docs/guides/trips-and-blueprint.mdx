A **trip** is the container for everything about one journey. Create one from the
home screen with an origin, destination, and dates; it is stored locally in
SQLite on your device.

## Editing and archiving a trip

Plans move. **Edit trip** opens a dialog to fix a trip's destination, dates, or
name — your imported documents, confirmed facts, and plans all stay exactly where
they were.

When a trip is over, **Archive** tucks it out of the way without deleting
anything, and **Unarchive** brings it straight back. Archived trips are hidden by
default; a **Show archived / Hide archived** toggle on the home screen reveals
them so you can reopen one whenever you need it.

## The Blueprint

Inside a trip, the **Blueprint** is a deterministic view of your confirmed
flights, stays, trains, coaches, ferries, and hire cars in itinerary order. It is
built only from facts you have confirmed — never from a model — so it always
reflects exactly what you have entered or reviewed.

Two deterministic checks run over the Blueprint and surface as advisory findings,
never as blockers:

- **Itinerary conflicts** — overlapping flights, overlapping lodging, and nights
  with no lodging coverage.
- **Readiness** — a plan-completeness rollup (schedule conflicts, lodging
  coverage, pending items to review) with an overall status. Sourced readiness
  (advisories, entry rules, health) is covered in
  [Readiness and official advice](/Voyalier/guides/readiness-and-advice/).

## Adding reservations

You can add a flight, stay, train, coach, ferry, or hire car two ways:

- **Manually**, by entering its details directly.
- **By importing a confirmation** and reviewing what Voyalier extracts — see
  [Importing confirmations](/Voyalier/guides/importing-confirmations/).

Imports accept a pasted confirmation or a local `.eml`/`.html`/`.txt` file you
pick or drag and drop; either way it is read on your device and nothing is
uploaded.

## The "Today" summary

At the top of an active trip, an offline **Today** summary shows where the trip
stands right now: its phase (upcoming, active, or completed, with day counts),
today's departures, arrivals, check-ins, and clearly separate traveler-authored
plans, plus the next anchor coming up. It is computed from local records against
the current date — no network, no model.

Each row and the next anchor offers **Show in Plan**. A confirmed departure,
arrival, or stay opens its exact fact; a traveler-authored activity or transfer
opens its exact plan row. The target stays in entry-scoped memory rather than the
URL. If the record was removed before the action completes, Voyalier opens Plan
and says the exact item is no longer available.

## If something slips

The disruption view is another deterministic projection of the confirmed plan.
It shows the time between related transport commitments, which later
commitments are stacked behind a leg, and operator names already present in
your own records. That can include a nearby rental pickup or return as well as
the next scheduled leg. **Show record** returns to the exact approved local fact
behind any of those rows.

This is not delay prediction or live operations data. Voyalier does not know
whether a service is late, whether another departure exists, or whether an
operator can help. It shows what the local plan depends on and where the
traveler's existing evidence lives.

## Find in this trip

**Find in this trip** searches across your imported documents, confirmed plans,
and saved research as you type. It is relaxed on purpose: partial words work,
and any word in your query can match — so "shuttle code" surfaces a result
mentioning either. Each match says whether it is an imported document, a
confirmed plan, or a research resource instead of lending one source another's
authority.

Matching terms are offered as **suggestion chips** you can tap to autofill the
query. **Copy** reuses the visible value (a confirmation code, a hotel name)
without retyping; **Show source** returns to the exact local document, confirmed
fact, or saved-reading row. That return does not reopen a sealed document body
or fetch a saved page. If the row was removed after the search, Voyalier opens
the right section and says the exact source is unavailable. The search query and
source identifier stay out of the URL. It is purely local — nothing leaves your
device.

The top-bar **Search workspace** action extends that local search across every
trip and also includes trip notes, saved places, and manual activities/transfers.
Each result states its trip and source type; pending extraction candidates stay
excluded until reviewed.

If you reload while a trip is open, Voyalier re-enters that trip and returns to
the selected Plan, Prepare, Discover, or AI section. The re-entry hint is only a
trip id in session storage; choosing **All trips** clears it.

## Sharing a redacted brief

**Share brief** produces a print-friendly summary of confirmed flights, stays,
surface journeys, and traveler-authored plans, including undated ideas. Their
titles and optional locations are shareable; private plan notes stay excluded.
You can print it, save it as a PDF, or choose **Copy brief** for readable plain text. Confirmation codes
and traveler names are excluded before the interface receives the brief; the
copy formatter then reads an allowlist that also has no imported text, research
resources, or private plan notes.

If the browser or operating system denies clipboard access, Voyalier says so and
keeps Print / Save as PDF available instead of claiming the copy succeeded.
After a successful copy, system clipboard history, device sync, or a clipboard
manager may retain the redacted text. Review the audience and clear the clipboard
when that matters.
