---
title: Prepare the travel-day kit
description: Keep reviewed confirmations, original files, transfers, and available offline packs close at hand.
---

Before departure, import final confirmations, link the useful originals, review
the next transfer, and download any available city pack. The cockpit reports
document counts and wallet size; Today uses confirmed evidence and authored
plans already on the device.

Online provider and authority links remain unavailable without a network.
Missing packs stay labeled as missing or links-only. A route line is a planning
aid and never substitutes for live directions, closures, or operator notices.
