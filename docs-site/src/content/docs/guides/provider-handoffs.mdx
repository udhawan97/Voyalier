---
title: Compare and book on a provider
description: Carry a clear brief to a provider, keep a return record, and import the final confirmation.
---

Copy the search brief, then choose **Prepare provider**. Voyalier saves a
Considering handoff before it reveals the external link. The card states which
fields the destination link carries and which filters still need entry.

On return, mark the candidate saved or record that you booked it. A
traveler-reported booking is still not confirmed evidence. Use **Import
confirmation**, review the extracted fields, and approve them before they reach
the itinerary.

Airbnb, Expedia, Yelp, Reddit, AllTrails, and Google links are link-only. Their
pages are not cached, indexed, sent to AI, or included in exports by Voyalier.
